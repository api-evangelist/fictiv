Dimensional drift and defects are the failure modes manufacturing engineers work hardest to minimize in injection molding. Initial sample parts measure accurately, the pilot run looks clean, and tooling is signed off for production. Months later, parts drift out of tolerance, or flash, sink marks, and short shots start appearing.
